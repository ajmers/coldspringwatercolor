About the Artist

Anne Maiale is inspired by clouds, light and shadows, her two children, and her cats.

She grew up surrounded by the watercolors of her uncle, Canadian artist Peter Schmidt, but didn't discover her own fascination with watercolor until many years later.

Prints and commissions available upon request.
